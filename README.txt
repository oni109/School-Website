EPIPHANY SCHOOLS WEBSITE CONCEPT
================================

HOW TO VIEW
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the epiphany-schools-demo folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open it in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
   You can also open the folder in Visual Studio Code and use Live Server.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- Responsive website for Nursery, Primary and Secondary sections
- About, academics, school values, news/events, admissions and contact sections
- Mobile navigation and demonstration enquiry form
- Proposed crest and provisional navy/blue/gold/green palette

IMPORTANT BEFORE OFFICIAL PUBLICATION
- The online search did not confirm an official Epiphany Schools logo or school website.
- The crest and palette are temporary presentation concepts, not official branding.
- Replace the stock photos with approved photographs of the actual school, pupils,
  facilities and activities (with appropriate permissions).
- Confirm the school's official name, motto, address, phone, email, fees, admission
  requirements, calendar, leadership names and all claims with school management.
- The enquiry form is front-end demonstration only. It does not send or save data.
  A backend/email integration is required for real enquiries.
- Stock images load from Unsplash and Google Fonts load online, so an internet
  connection is needed for those remote assets.
