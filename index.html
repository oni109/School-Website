Welcome to Epiphany Schools

Education with purpose, care and a strong foundation.

Epiphany Schools brings nursery, primary and secondary education together in one learning journey. Our proposed website presents a school community committed to helping every child develop academically, socially, morally and spiritually.

Guided by Catholic educational values, the school experience can unite sound teaching, personal responsibility, respect for others and a lifelong love of learning.
